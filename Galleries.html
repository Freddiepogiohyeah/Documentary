Realities We Can’t Ignore

This document sheds light on pressing issues plaguing our nation. From corruption and unequal justice, where fairness serves only the wealthy, to the tragic loss of children due to a broken healthcare system. It also portrays the sorrowful fate of missing children, many of whom fall into the wrong paths due to neglect and poverty. Through powerful imagery, this project aims to open eyes, spark conversations, and inspire action toward a more just and compassionate society.
