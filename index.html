Documentaries

Justice for the Rich, Prison for the Poor.

On this picture captured, it shows the reality of a system built for the wealthy. Justice is bought, not earned. The rich walk free, using their power and connections, while the poor face severe punishment for minor offenses. A man who steals food to survive is jailed, yet those who steal millions remain untouched. The law favors the privileged, letting them abuse power without consequence. For the poor, it’s not just injustice; it’s survival in a world where fairness is a luxury they can't afford.

They Steal, We Kneel.

This picture captured a family's quiet prayer, seeking peace in a nation burdened by corruption. While those in power steal what’s meant for the people, families like this are left with only faith. They kneel not from privilege, but desperation. Corruption isn’t just in headlines; it’s in empty plates, broken roads, and shattered hopes. The rich make promises they never keep, while the poor remain in the same streets, holding on to prayers instead of change. We work, we hope, we suffer, but they steal, and still, we kneel.

Empty Hands, Heavy Hearts

This picture shows the light in the eyes of children, smiling despite the weight they carry. In the Philippines, where the rich thrive and the poor survive, these kids smile from courage, not comfort. Hunger, sickness, and despair follow them, but they face each day with hope. Poverty here isn’t just financial but also it’s the lack of opportunity and the struggle against a system that holds them back. Yet, even with empty hands, their hearts are full of dreams, and that smile is their defiance.

Addiction's Cost, Freedom Lost.

The image captured the harsh reality of the drug crisis in the Philippines. A suspect, handcuffed and helpless, stands as a symbol of countless lives lost in the grip of addiction. While law enforcement fights to reclaim the streets, the real battle is the one for the soul of a nation caught between hope and despair. Drugs destroy not just lives, but entire communities, leaving the poor with no escape. The cost is high, and freedom, once lost, is hard to regain.

Shattered Rights, Broken Lights.

On this image captured, it shows the painful reality of human rights in the Philippines. The bound, lifeless figure in the image reflects the lives lost in the midst of extrajudicial violence, a tragic outcome of the country’s war on drugs. It highlights how human rights are often ignored in the pursuit of justice, raising questions about due process and the respect for dignity in a system that too often overlooks the value of life.

Under the Sun, Work Never Done.

On this picture, it shows the harsh reality of construction workers toiling under the blazing sun. Their days are filled with sweat, strain, and unrelenting hours of labor. In the Philippines, if you haven’t finished school, you’re left with few options but to work hard in physically demanding jobs just to survive. As a result, a gallon of sweat becomes the price for survival, and rest is a luxury that few can afford. They push through the pain, knowing that each day is another struggle for survival, yet still hoping for a brighter future that always feels just beyond their grasp.

About This Project

Through my lens, I uncover the harsh realities of Philippine society, bringing to light the untold stories and hidden struggles that shape our everyday lives. This project is more than just photography—it is a visual narrative of injustice, resilience, and the unfiltered truth. Every image captured serves as a voice for those who are often unheard, a testament to the realities that many choose to ignore. By exposing these moments, I hope to spark awareness, ignite conversations, and inspire action toward meaningful change.

This project is purely for academic purposes and is not intended to harm or offend anyone. I happened to come across some images and documents on social media, and it just so happened that I used them as part of this project. All the photos included here were also sourced from the internet. I do not claim ownership of any of the images used.

I’m open for collaboration and feedbacks. Feel free to get in touch.
